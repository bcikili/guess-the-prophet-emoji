Guess the Islamic Prophets

A fun emoji guessing adventure through the stories of the Prophets in the Holy Qur'an & Sunnah!

🎮 How to Play & Game Rules

  1. 1
    Choose Your Level: Pick Easy, Medium, or Advanced below. You will get 10 random questions from that level!
  2. 2
    Look at the Emojis First: Each round starts by showing big, colorful emojis on the screen. Try to guess the Prophet right from the emojis!
  3. 3
    Click "Next" for the Question: Click the Next button in the bottom-right corner and the full Question clue will appear on screen!
  4. 4
    Click "Answer" to Reveal: Click the Answer button to see the Name of the Prophet and the Context: behind the story!
